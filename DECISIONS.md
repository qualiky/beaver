## BeaverCore engine decisions

- **Own date maths.** `LocalDate` uses integer civil-date algorithms (proleptic Gregorian, Monday = first weekday, ISO weekday 1...7). No `Calendar` or `TimeZone` is involved in arithmetic, so DST cannot move shifts. A `Calendar` is only used by `LocalDate.today(in:)` and `date(in:)` at the UI edge.
- **IDs are `Int`; withholding table IDs are `String`** (built-in is `"au-scale2-weekly"`). `TaxMethod.withholdingTable(id:)` looks in custom tables first, then built-ins; a missing table gives zero tax.
- **Exact coefficients.** Withholding `a` and `b` are stored as integers in ten-thousandths, `belowX` as whole dollars (`nil` = no limit). `x < belowX` compares `floor(gross dollars)` since `belowX` is a whole number.
- **Tax frequency.** Tables are weekly. Fortnightly applies the table to `floor(gross/2)` and doubles. Monthly (not in the spec) converts to a weekly equivalent (gross x 12/52), applies the table, and scales the tax back (x 52/12). Flat percent is stored in basis points.
- **Hours.** Hours caps and the fortnight limit are whole hours (Int), compared with paid minutes (break removed) of non-cancelled shifts, counted on the shift's start date. A fortnight limit is active only for fortnights starting on or after the Monday of `effectiveFrom`; it reports `.hard`.
- **Band hours** are stored as hundredths of an hour (Int), rounded half up from the exact total over the period. Minutes no rule covers are priced at zero under the band "Unrated". A break larger than the shift is clamped to the shift; `end == start` is a 24 hour shift.
- **Bills.** The anchor is always the first occurrence. For `monthly(dayOfMonth:)` later occurrences use that day (clamped); `quarterly` and `yearly` keep the anchor's day (clamped, computed from the anchor each time). A paid occurrence keeps its own paid amount, so editing a bill's amount changes unpaid occurrences only; past unpaid occurrences also take the new amount (no amount history).
- **Templates.** The template for a week is the one with the latest `effectiveFrom` <= that week's Monday (nil = always). Template shifts are skipped by shift start date on unavailable days. A pay period "has shifts" if any shift row exists in it (including cancelled); only an empty period falls back to the template, and only when its payday is today or later or a pay is logged for it.
- **Forecast.** Window = Monday of today through the deadline's week (26 weeks with no goal deadline). Events and bills after the deadline are excluded; living counts for every window week using the budget. Primary goal = `primaryGoalID`, else the first goal. Without a goal, target is zero and `gap` equals the projected surplus. Required per payday is rounded half up, never negative, and counts distinct paydays from today to the deadline. Spending variance only counts past weeks that have recorded spending. Pay variance compares every logged pay with its expected pay (template used when the period has no shifts).
- **Suggested split.** Uses the next payday and the next distinct payday across active sources. Bills = unpaid occurrences from the next payday up to the day before the following one. Living = budget x days / 7. Sinking fund = annual cost of non-weekly, non-one-off bills x days / 365. Items are funded in priority order from the expected pay; the unfunded total is `fromBuffer`, any leftover is `buffer`.

## M2 data layer

- **Layout.** `Beaver/Data/`: `Database.swift` (`AppDatabase`: `DatabasePool` at Application Support/`beaver.sqlite`, in-memory `DatabaseQueue` factory, foreign keys on), `Migrations.swift` (`v1_initial`), `Records/`, `Repository.swift` (all runtime SQL), `Backup.swift`, `AppStore.swift`. Views never import GRDB.
- **No BeaverCore changes.** Records map to the existing public types.
- **Schema deviations from SPEC §7.** Column types: dates and times are ISO `TEXT`; money is `INTEGER` cents (`*_cents`); booleans are `INTEGER` 0/1. `income_source.active` / `bill.active` map to `isActive`. `withholding_table.id` is `TEXT` (the engine uses string ids). Added `withholding_table.note` (the built-in table has a "verify against ATO" note). `rate_card_id` is `ON DELETE SET NULL`; every child table (`template`, `shift`, `pay_received`, `bill_payment`, `deposit`, `template_shift`) is `ON DELETE CASCADE`. `week_spend_total.week_start` is always a Monday. Added `CHECK`s on `kind`, `status`, `weekday`, and indexes on `shift` and `expense` dates. Weekly caps are columns `weekly_cap_soft` / `weekly_cap_hard` on `income_source`.
- **JSON columns** (`rules_json`, `rows_json`, `schedule_json`, `tax_method_json`, `cadence_json`) hold the Codable BeaverCore values (`[RateRule]`, `[WithholdingRow]`, `PaySchedule`, `TaxMethod`, `Cadence`), written by GRDB's Codable record support. Dates inside records are plain strings, never JSON-quoted.
- **Ids.** Core value types use id 0 for "new"; the record gets `nil` and SQLite `AUTOINCREMENT` assigns one. A non-zero id is stored as given (demo data and import keep their ids). Every `save` returns the stored value. `BillPayment` has no id in the engine; the table keeps one for stability, and `(bill_id, due_date)` is the real key (marking paid twice replaces).
- **Built-in withholding table** is inserted by `v1_initial` with `builtin = 1`. It is reference data, can not be edited or deleted (`DataError.builtinReadOnly`), is excluded from `ForecastInput.withholdingTables` (the engine already has it), and survives demo load, wipe and import (re-inserted if a backup lacks it).
- **Settings** are JSON fragments in `setting`: `currency`, `timeZone`, `onboardingDone`, `lastExportAt` (ISO 8601 string), `primaryGoalId`, `livingBudgetCents`. Missing keys read as defaults (AUD, device time zone, false, nil, nil, $0), so a fresh database has zero rows.
- **Demo data** sets `onboardingDone = true`, currency AUD, the living budget and the primary goal, because the demo is a complete setup. Nothing is ever seeded at launch.
- **Backup.** One JSON file: `schemaVersion` (1), `appVersion`, `exportedAt` (ISO 8601), `tables` with one array per table (camelCase table keys, record fields as stored). Export writes `lastExportAt` and reads all tables in one write transaction, so the file contains the new timestamp. Import reads only `schemaVersion` first, so a future file is refused with a version message before its shape matters. Versions other than 1 (newer, zero or negative) throw `BackupError.unsupportedVersion`; non-JSON throws `.notABackup`; bad content or a broken foreign key throws `.corrupt`. The replace runs in one transaction and rolls back fully on any failure.
- **AppStore.** `@Observable @MainActor`. Reads the database synchronously at init (no onboarding flash), then `run()` follows one `ValueObservation` over settings plus the engine input and recomputes `Forecast`. "Today" comes from the settings time zone via an injectable clock; `refreshToday()` moves the window after midnight. The what-if `extraWeeklyIncome` is in memory only.
- **AppModel** exposes `onboardingDone` from the persisted setting, `completeOnboarding()`, and `AppModel.live()` (falls back to an in-memory database and sets `startupError` if the file can not be opened).

## M3 Week and Plan tabs

- **Logic lives in view models.** `WeekViewModel` and `PlanViewModel` are plain structs over `Forecast`, `ForecastInput` and `AppSettings` (no SwiftUI), so they are unit-tested in `BeaverTests/WeekPlanTests.swift`. Money uses `FigureFormatter` (narrow currency symbol, so AUD shows "$"); the locale is injectable for tests.
- **Headline.** Ahead is "+$X ahead", behind is "-$X behind" (signed, mirrored); a gap of exactly zero reads "Right on target". With no goal: "This week: +$X surplus" (or "-$X shortfall") from the current week's surplus. Status colours are custom light/dark values (the system green and red are too pale as text) and are always paired with a symbol.
- **"Bills due before the next payday"** = occurrences from this week's Monday to the day before the next payday (the suggested split's payday, i.e. on or after today), plus unpaid occurrences from the previous 8 weeks, shown as overdue. A bill due on the payday itself belongs to that payday's split, not to this list. Paid bills stay listed with a tick; ticking again calls `unmarkPaid`. Marking paid uses today's date and the planned amount.
- **Living budget left** = budget minus `Spending.spent` for the current week (a week total replaces entries). Quick-add expenses are dated today.
- **Danger banners** cover only the current week and the next one (`weeks.prefix(2)` with surplus < 0) and list that week's bills.
- **Fortnight hours bar.** The hours limits are not part of `ForecastInput`, so the Week tab loads them with `Repository.hoursLimits()` (on appear, when shifts change and when Settings closes). Used hours count all non-cancelled shift rows in the fortnight, rostered future ones included, but not template-generated shifts. If several limits apply, the one with the latest `effectiveFrom` wins. Amber from limit minus 4 hours up to the limit, red above it; the banner appears in the same two states.
- **Backup reminder** is due when `lastExportAt` is nil or more than 7 days (not 7 days exactly) old. `WeekView(onBackUp:)` takes the action closure; it defaults to a no-op until Settings supplies export.
- **Plan table** is a `List` with two-line rows (week and paydays; income tagged "actual" or "est.", bills, living) plus a trailing surplus and running balance. The balance is goal deposits so far plus the running total of weekly surplus. The what-if living budget stepper writes through `Repository.setLivingBudget` in $10 steps; extra income uses `AppStore.extraWeeklyIncome` in $25 steps (memory only). "Reset what-ifs" clears the extra income and restores the living budget to the value it had when the Plan tab was first shown (or when Settings last closed). Template choice is not offered as a what-if (not in the task).
- **Startup error banner.** `RootView` shows a non-dismissible red banner above the whole app (onboarding included) while `AppModel.startupError` is set, saying changes will not be saved.
## Roster tab (BEAVER-25 to 28)

- **Layout.** `Beaver/Features/Roster/`: `RosterViewModel` (all logic, testable against an in-memory `Repository`), `ShiftEditor` (editor model and form), `LogPayView` (model and form), `RosterFormat` (chips, hours, money parsing), `RosterView` (UI). The view model reloads after each write and when `AppStore.input` changes, so Settings edits show up.
- **Band chips** are derived from the rate card's band names by keyword: holiday = PH, Saturday = Sat, Sunday = Sun, evening = Eve, night = Night, day = Day; any other name uses its first word (up to 7 characters). Holiday is checked first because "holiday", "Saturday" and "Sunday" all contain "day". A separate orange chip shows the public holiday name when a shift starts or finishes on one.
- **Footers** per source that has shifts in the week. Hours and gross are for the week. For a weekly source tax and net are for the week. For a fortnightly source tax and net come from the whole pay period (tax is applied once on the period gross), with a note that says so.
- **Hours warnings** show inline in the footer (weekly caps) and in an "Hours limit" section (fortnight limit: the latest limit effective on or before the week's Sunday). They never block saving.
- **Copy last week** copies non-cancelled hourly shifts +7 days as rostered. It skips unavailable dates and any shift whose (source, date, start) already exists in the target week. **Apply template** uses `TemplateExpander` (the template in force for each date's week) with the same skip rules. Both ask for confirmation first (the dialog says how many shifts would be added) and report the result in a toast.
- **Save week as template** saves each source's non-cancelled shifts as a template effective from that week's Monday ("Week of d MMM"). A template already saved for the same source and Monday is replaced, not duplicated. This changes the forecast from that week on, and the confirmation says so.
- **Fast-path defaults.** A new shift takes start, end and break from the source's most recently created shift (highest id); otherwise 09:00 to 17:00 with no break. Changing the source in the add form re-applies that source's defaults. The date defaults to today if it is in the week shown, else that week's Monday.
- **Delete** is immediate with a 6 second Undo toast (the shift is re-saved with its old id).
- **Log pay.** Any active source can be chosen. Expected net for a payday = shifts in its work period (the template if the period has none, as in the forecast) taxed once on the period gross; fixed sources use their amount (less tax unless it is net). Difference = actual net - expected net (green with an up arrow when above, red with a down arrow when below). Logging the same source and payday again updates the existing row. Payday choices run from 180 days back to 7 days ahead.
## Editors (Beaver/Features/Editors)

- **Shape.** Each editor is a SwiftUI view over a small `@MainActor @Observable` view model that holds text fields as text, validates them, builds the BeaverCore value and saves through `Repository`. Views take an optional existing value, an `onSave` callback (receives the stored value) and, where it applies, `onDelete`. `style: .push` (default, needs a parent `NavigationStack`) or `.sheet` (wraps itself in a stack and adds Cancel).
- **Validation.** Every invalid field shows a red inline message. Save stays disabled until the whole form is valid. A rate card with weekday gaps only warns (spec 2.2 says show gaps, not block).
- **Money entry.** `MoneyParser` turns text into integer cents with no `Double`. It accepts "12", "12.5", ".5", "12.", "$12" and "1,234.56" (a comma must group exactly three digits) and rejects empty text, signs, three or more decimals and more than 12 whole digits. The decimal separator is always a dot, whatever the locale. Percentages reuse it: "12.5" is 1250 basis points.
- **Rate card inside an income source.** The embedded card has no name field and is saved as "<source name> rates". Editing a source updates its existing card in place. Deleting a source also deletes its card when no other source uses it. A new hourly source starts with no rates until the user taps "Start from Typical casual (template)".
- **Schedules.** Monthly is offered for fixed sources only (switching a source to hourly moves it to weekly). Lag weeks are shown for hourly sources only (0 to 8, default 1); fixed sources keep lag 1. A fixed source entered as net hides the tax section and saves `.none`.
- **Presets.** `BillPreset` holds a name, a category and a `BillCadenceKind`, never an amount. A monthly preset takes its day from the next-due date. Changing the next-due date of a monthly bill moves the day of month with it.
- **Goals.** "Already saved" appears only for a new goal and writes one `Deposit` dated today in the settings time zone. "Make primary" writes `primaryGoalId`; the first goal defaults to primary. Goal, deposit and setting are written in separate repository calls, not one transaction.
- **Limits.** `LimitsEditor` edits one value (fortnight limit, holidays, unavailable ranges) and writes the difference on Save, so Cancel discards everything. It can't save before the stored values have loaded, so it never wipes data by mistake. Only one fortnight limit is kept: Save deletes the others. Unavailable rows added in the editor use negative temporary ids until saved.
- **Default weeks.** `TemplateEditor` edits one template; `TemplatesEditor(sourceID:)` lists, adds, edits and deletes all of a source's templates. Shifts are saved ordered by weekday then start time. A start equal to the end is rejected (the engine would read it as 24 hours); an end before the start means the shift finishes next day.

Permission is asked only when it is turned on. `PayReminderPlanner.reminders` is pure: one reminder per distinct payday from tomorrow to 28 days ahead, fired at 20:00 on the day before (skipped if that time has passed today). Body: "Pay day tomorrow: expected $X. Bills due before next payday: $Y." where X is the summed net of that payday's pay events and Y the unpaid bills from the payday up to the next payday. `payReminderSync()` on `MainTabView` reschedules whenever the forecast changes. Erase does not clear scheduled reminders; the next forecast change replaces them.
- **Re-run setup** presents `OnboardingLauncher` full screen. It shows `OnboardingView()` until the Onboarding branch lands; then switch it to `OnboardingView(mode: .rerun)`.
- **Preferences.** Currency is a short list (AUD, NZD, USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, SGD, JPY); the time zone is any known identifier; the living budget saves on submit or when focus leaves.
## Money tab

- **Layout.** `Beaver/Features/Money/`: `MoneyViewModel.swift` (pure models `BillsModel`, `SpendingModel`, `GoalsModel`, the sheet forms and `MoneyActions` for writes), `BillsSection`, `SpendingSection`, `GoalsSection`, `MoneySheets`. The models are built from `AppStore.input`, so the tab updates with the database. No BeaverCore or other-folder changes.
- **Bill grouping.** The window is this week's Monday for 8 weeks. A bill due on a payday goes under that payday's "Before ‹payday›" header (the first payday on or after the due date), so Rent on 2026-11-25 sits under the 25 Nov group. This differs from the Week tab's "due on the payday belongs to the next split" rule; the Money list is a "pay for these by that payday" view. Paydays come from active income sources only. With none, groups are weeks ("This week", "Week of Nov 30"). Group totals use paid amounts for paid rows.
- **Overdue** = unpaid occurrences of active bills in the 8 weeks before today, pinned above the groups and not repeated in them. Paid occurrences earlier this week stay in their group with a tick.
- **Monthly view** lists active bills; monthly, quarterly, yearly and one-off bills sort by day of the month (the anchor's day for the last three); weekly, fortnightly and every-N-days bills follow, sorted by name, with their cadence text.
- **Amount change gap.** Verified: a paid occurrence keeps the amount in its `BillPayment`, so editing a bill's amount never changes paid history (covered by a test). Not met: the spec says an edit applies "from the next occurrence on", but the core has no amount history, so every unpaid occurrence, including overdue ones, takes the new amount. Fixing it needs an effective-from amount in BeaverCore.
- **Spending history.** Budget is the current living budget for all 12 weeks (no budget history). The running variance total sums completed weeks that have recorded spending, oldest first; weeks with nothing logged are skipped, and the current week is left out as it is incomplete. A week total is stored against the Monday; entries are kept but dimmed and ignored while it is set.
- **Goals.** The primary badge follows the forecast rule: `primaryGoalID`, else the first goal. "Make primary" is hidden on the primary goal.
## Onboarding (BEAVER-42 to 46)

- **Layout.** `Beaver/Features/Onboarding/`: `OnboardingModel` (steps, settings, restore, summary, weekly maths; no views), `OnboardingView` (`mode: .firstRun | .rerun`, chrome, sheets, file importer) and `OnboardingSteps` (one view per step). The view injects the `AppStore` and repository into the environment itself, so a Settings sheet needs nothing extra.
- **Editors save themselves.** The flow only opens them as sheets and then reloads (`refresh()`), so there is one write path. The flow writes only currency, time zone, the living budget and the finish flag.
- **Navigation.** Seven steps. Back, Next and Skip are always in the bottom bar; Skip is hidden on Welcome and Summary. Next on Welcome saves the currency and time zone (so the device defaults are stored, not only the AUD fallback); Next on Living money saves the budget; Skip never writes. A typed but invalid budget blocks Next, not Skip. An empty budget leaves the stored value alone.
- **Defaults.** First run: currency from the device locale (added to the list when it is not a common code) and the device time zone. Re-run: the stored values and existing data.
- **Restore.** `fileImporter` (JSON) stages the data; an in-app confirmation dialog applies it with `Backup.import`. Success sets `onboardingDone` (also when the file was exported mid-setup) and finishes; failure shows `BackupError.localizedDescription` and the import's own transaction leaves the database untouched.
- **Weekly equivalents.** Integer cents, round half up: fortnightly /2, every N days x7/N, monthly x12/52, quarterly x4/52, yearly /52, one-off 0. Inactive bills are skipped. Income per week: fixed sources use net per pay (tax by the source's method) scaled the same way; hourly sources price their default week (the next two weeks for fortnightly, tax once on the period gross). An hourly source with no default week counts as zero.
- **Summary.** Uses `AppStore.forecast` (reloaded after every change). With a goal: "$X ahead", "$X behind by <deadline>" or "Right on target". Without: "Weekly surplus ≈ $X" (or "Weekly shortfall") from income minus bills minus living, not from a single forecast week, because pay timing makes one week misleading.
- **Salary preconfiguration.** `IncomeSourceEditor` has no kind parameter, so "Salary / fixed amount" opens it with a blank fixed draft (id 0, saved as new). The editor then reads "Edit income" and shows a Delete row. Hourly opens with `source: nil` (its default kind). A `kind:` initializer would remove both warts.
- **Usual week.** After a new hourly source is saved, an alert offers "Set your usual week (optional)" and opens `TemplatesEditor` as a sheet. Hourly rows also have a "Usual week" swipe action.

### Kotlin engine port (BEAVER-57 to 59)

- **Layout.** `android/core/src/main/kotlin/au/com/fuzn/beaver/core/` mirrors the Swift files one to one (`Money`, `RateCard`, `ShiftPay`, `Withholding`, `PaySchedule`, `IncomeSource`, `Bills`, `HoursLimits`, `Spending`, `Goals`, `Forecast`, `Split`, `DemoData`), plus `Dates.kt` (date helpers and serializers) and `Json.kt`.
- **Dates.** Swift's own `LocalDate`/`LocalTime`/`Weekday` become `java.time.LocalDate`, `LocalTime` and `DayOfWeek` (ISO 1 to 7, Monday first). Only date-only maths is used (`plusDays`, `plusMonths`, `toEpochDay`); no instants, zones or `Clock`. `plusMonths` clamps to the month end like the Swift `adding(months:)`. Swift's `mondayOfWeek`, `sundayOfWeek`, `previousSunday`, `week` and `days(until:)` are extension members (`daysUntil`). `parseIsoDate`/`parseIsoTime` are strict (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `HH:mm`, so `24:00` and `2026-2-3` fail).
- **Money.** `@JvmInline value class Money(val cents: Long)`; other counts (minutes, ids, basis points, ten-thousandths) are `Int` and widened to `Long` inside the maths. `IntMath.roundHalfUp(n, d)` is `floorDiv(2n + d, 2d)`, the same as Swift, so ties go toward positive infinity.
- **Exact band hours.** Swift sums band hours in `Decimal`. Kotlin keeps an exact rational (`BigInteger` numerator and denominator) per band and rounds half up to hundredths once at the end. This equals the fixture and the Swift result for every case; it can only differ from `Decimal` where `Decimal`'s 38-digit division would have rounded a repeating fraction.
- **Naming.** Sealed Swift enums become sealed interfaces: `PaySchedule.Weekly/Fortnightly/Monthly`, `TaxMethod.None/FlatPercent/Table` (Swift `.withholdingTable(id:)`), `Cadence.Weekly ... OneOff`. Swift enum case names are kept as lowercase Kotlin enum entries (`DaySelector.weekdays`, `ShiftStatus.rostered`, `SplitKind.sinkingFund`) so the serial names match without annotations. Swift's `workPeriod(forPayday:)` and `payday(forWorkDate:)` are `workPeriod(payday)` and `paydayForWorkDate(date)`. `WithholdingTable.auScale2WeeklyID` is `AU_SCALE2_WEEKLY_ID`; the built-in id is still `"au-scale2-weekly"`.
- **Backup JSON compatibility.** Persisted models are `@Serializable` with the Swift property names (`sourceID`, `rateCardID`, `aTenThousandths` and so on). `Money` is a bare integer, dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, times `HH:mm`, weekdays the ISO integer. Swift's synthesized enum shape is reproduced by custom serializers: `{"weekly":{"payWeekday":3,"lagWeeks":1}}`, `{"none":{}}`, `{"everyNDays":{"_0":28}}`, `{"oneOff":{}}`. These serializers only work with the JSON format. Use `BeaverJson` (`encodeDefaults`, `explicitNulls = false`, `ignoreUnknownKeys`) so nil optionals are omitted, as Swift does. Result types (`Forecast`, `PeriodPay`, `ShiftPayResult`, `SuggestedSplit`, `HoursWarning`) are plain data classes, not serializable, because nothing persists them.
- **Tests.** Gradle passes the absolute path of `fixtures/golden.json` (resolved from `android/core`) as the system property `beaver.fixture.path` and declares the file as a test input, so the tests always read the shared file and re-run when it changes. The loader in `Fixture.kt` mirrors `Fixture.swift`. A parity test asserts `DemoData.forecastInput()` equals the fixture's forecast input field by field (the only difference is the fixture's custom copy of the built-in table, which is compared separately), not just the totals.

### Android data layer (BEAVER-60, 61)

- **Room schema v1 mirrors iOS `v1_initial`.** Same table and column names and types (text dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, times `HH:mm`, integer cents, `*_json` text columns holding the core values in the iOS JSON shape), same foreign keys and `ON DELETE` actions, same index names (`shift_on_date`, `shift_on_source_date`, `expense_on_date`) and `UNIQUE(bill_id, due_date)`. Schema JSON is exported to `android/app/schemas/` and committed. Never edit version 1; add a version and a migration. Room cannot express SQLite `CHECK` constraints, so the iOS checks (`kind`, `status`, weekday 1..7) are enforced in code: rows are converted to core values, which rejects bad enums, dates and times, and import validates every row before writing.
- **Database file** is `beaver.db` in `noBackupFilesDir` (never in cloud backup). The built-in AU Scale 2 table (`builtin = 1`) is inserted in `RoomDatabase.Callback.onCreate`, is read-only through the repository (`DataError.BuiltinReadOnly`), and survives `eraseAll()`/`loadDemoData()`. Import deletes every row, as iOS does, so the backup's copy of the built-in table is used; if the backup lacks one, the code's table is inserted.
- **Entities double as backup records.** Each entity is `@Serializable` with the Swift record property names (camelCase: `rateCardId`, `rulesJson`, `breakMin`, `amountCents`), so one class is both the Room row and the backup JSON. `JsonTextSerializer` writes a `*_json` string column as a nested JSON value and reads it back, matching how Swift's Codable records encode `[RateRule]`, `PaySchedule` and so on. Ids default to 0 (Room assigns the next id); null optionals are omitted (`BeaverJson`).
- **Backup document** is exactly iOS: `schemaVersion`, `appVersion`, `exportedAt` (ISO 8601 UTC whole seconds), `tables` with the 17 camelCase table keys. Export writes pretty JSON and records `lastExportAt` (a setting, JSON-encoded string) in the same transaction. Import checks `schemaVersion == 1` first (`BackupError.UnsupportedVersion`, `NotABackup`, `Corrupt`, same messages as iOS), then replaces all data in one Room transaction; any failure rolls back. Android is more lenient than iOS on one point: a missing table key reads as empty (iOS's synthesized decoder requires every key).
- **No format mismatches** were found between the Kotlin core serializers and the iOS export, so `android/core` is unchanged. Proof: Android imports `fixtures/demo-backup-v1.json` (an iOS export) to the golden gap, and its re-export equals that file semantically (only `exportedAt` and `appVersion` ignored). The test passes the file's own `exportedAt` as `now` so the `lastExportAt` setting inside the tables is equal too.
- **Repository** is the iOS API in Kotlin with `suspend` functions (no main-thread blocking): `save(x)` overloads return the stored value with its id, `deleteX(id)`, `markPaid`/`unmarkPaid`, `setWeekSpendTotal` (stored against the Monday), settings getters and setters, `forecastInput(today)`, `hoursLimits()`, `loadDemoData()`, `eraseAll()`. Saves use Room `@Upsert` (never `REPLACE`, which would cascade-delete children). All SQL is in `BeaverDao` and `Repository`; the DAO is only used by the repository.
- **Observation** uses `InvalidationTracker.createFlow`, re-reading on any change (`observeSnapshot` watches all tables).
- **AppStore** is created with `AppStore.create(repository, scope, clock)`, which reads the current state first (no onboarding flash) then follows the database in the given scope. It exposes `StateFlow`s `settings`, `input`, `forecast`, `lastError` and `extraWeeklyIncome` (an in-memory what-if, not saved; set with `setExtraWeeklyIncome`).
- **Tests.** `:app` unit tests use Robolectric 4.17 (SDK 35 via `robolectric.properties`) with in-memory Room, JUnit 4.13.2, androidx.test core 1.7.0 and kotlinx-coroutines 1.10.2 (all pinned in the version catalog). Gradle passes `fixtures/demo-backup-v1.json` as `beaver.fixture.backup` and declares it a test input. Optional private check: `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest -Pbeaver.private.backup=/path/to/backup.json` imports that file into a throwaway in-memory database and prints the gap and payday count; without the property the test is skipped. Never commit a private backup (`*.backup.json` is git-ignored).

### Android editors (BEAVER-62)

- **Where.** `android/app/src/main/kotlin/au/com/fuzn/beaver/ui/editors/`. Each editor is a full-screen composable (a `Scaffold` with Cancel and Save) taking `repository`, an optional existing value, `onSaved` and `onDismiss` (plus `onDeleted` where the iOS editor has Delete). There is no navigation library and no `CompositionLocal` for the repository yet, so the repository is a plain parameter. Callers show an editor as a destination or in a dialog window as they like. Public: `MoneyField`, `MoneyParser`, `IncomeSourceEditor`, `RateCardEditor` (and `RateCardSections` for embedding), `TemplateEditor`, `TemplatesEditor`, `BillEditor`, `BillPresetPicker`, `GoalEditor`, `LimitsEditor`. Shared helpers other screens may use: `MoneyParser` (parse, `text`, `display`, `symbol`), `EditorLabels` (weekday, category and date text), `EditorScaffold`.
- **Logic is in plain state holders** (`RateCardEditorModel`, `IncomeSourceEditorModel`, `TemplateEditorModel`, `TemplatesListModel`, `BillEditorModel`, `GoalEditorModel`, `LimitsEditorModel`). They hold text fields as text on Compose snapshot state, validate, build the core value and save through `Repository` with `suspend` functions. They are not `ViewModel`s (no lifecycle dependency), so the JVM tests drive them directly. Known gap: the models are `remember`ed, so a half-filled form is lost on a configuration change (rotation, font size change). Hoisting them into a retained holder is a follow-up.
- **Validation matches iOS.** A field shows its error only after it has been edited or a save was attempted (`FieldTouches`, `revealAll`). A rate card with weekday gaps warns and never blocks.

- **One rate card per hourly source.** The embedded card model starts with the source's `rateCardID`, `load` adopts the stored rules, and `save` upserts under that id. Save also stays disabled until `load` has finished. A new hourly source creates exactly one card, named "<source name> rates". Switching an hourly source to fixed leaves the old card in place (unchanged from iOS).
- **Table tax.** Choosing "Table" selects the first stored table until another is picked (iOS only does this on load).
- **Monthly bills.** The day of the month follows the next due date: changing the due date of a monthly bill sets the day to the new date's day, and switching a bill to monthly takes the day from the due date. An existing monthly bill keeps its stored day when opened, even if its first due date was clamped to a short month.
- **Presets.** 13 `BillPreset`s, each with its own Material icon (house, smartphone, wifi, bolt, local_fire_department, water_drop, directions_bus, fitness_center, tv, music_note, cloud, shield, credit_card). Every chip uses the same `labelLarge` text style, a minimum height of 48dp and up to two lines; chips in a row share the height of the tallest, and the column count is `width / 148dp`.
- **Dates and times.** Material 3 `DatePickerDialog` and `TimePicker` (24-hour) behind labelled fields. The date picker works in UTC milliseconds, so `LocalDate` converts through UTC and never shifts a day. Dropdowns are plain `DropdownMenu`s, not `ExposedDropdownMenuBox`.
- **Default weeks.** `TemplatesEditor` shows its list and the `TemplateEditor` inside one destination (state, plus a back handler), ordered by `effectiveFrom` with "always" first.
- **Previews** use `DemoData` (the fictional "Sam") or empty models only; they call the stateless `*Form` composables, so no database is needed.
- **Tests** (`ui/editors`, 74): `MoneyParser` cases, per-editor validation including the pristine form showing no errors, and repository round trips on in-memory Room (no second rate card after repeated edits, monthly day follows the due date, goal deposit and primary setting, limits keep a single limit and delete removed rows).
